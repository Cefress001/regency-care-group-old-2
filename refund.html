Refund Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026
30 days, full refund, no questions asked. If SellerKit Pro isn't worth what you paid, email us within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund every cent.

What's covered

Any SellerKit Pro purchase is refundable in full for 30 days from the date of purchase. You do not need to explain why. You do not need to prove you used the tools. There is no form to fill in and no retention offer to sit through.

How to request one

Email support@sellerkit.io from the address you used at checkout, with the word "refund" in the subject line. That's the whole process.

If you no longer have access to that email address, include your name, the approximate purchase date, and the last four digits of the card you used so we can find the transaction.

How long it takes

  • We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days
  • We submit the refund to Stripe within 5 business days of your request
  • Your bank then takes a further 5–10 business days to post it to your statement — that part is out of our hands

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

What happens to your access

Your access to the toolkit ends when the refund is issued. Anything you already produced with SellerKit Pro — listings, titles, descriptions, exported files — is yours to keep and use.

Data saved in your browser is not deleted by a refund. To remove it, clear your browser's site data for this site.

After 30 days

We are not obliged to refund after the 30-day window has closed. That said, if something has genuinely gone wrong — the service was unavailable, a tool was broken, or you were charged twice — email us anyway. We would rather sort it out than have you feel cheated.

Duplicate and accidental charges

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, email us and we will refund the duplicate immediately, regardless of how long ago it happened.

Before you file a chargeback

If you are unhappy, please email us first. A refund from us is faster than a bank dispute — usually days rather than weeks — and we will not argue with you about it. Chargebacks cost both of us time that a one-line email would have saved.
