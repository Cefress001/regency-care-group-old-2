Common Scam Patterns

Overpayment Check

Buyer sends a check for MORE than the price, asks you to deposit and wire back the difference. Check bounces days later — you lose the item and the money.

Fake Payment Screenshot

Buyer sends a Venmo/PayPal screenshot that looks real. Only release an item when the payment shows as completed in YOUR actual account — never based on a screenshot.

Shipping Scam

Buyer wants you to ship first, pays "after you ship." You ship, payment never arrives. Full payment before shipping, always — no exceptions.

Google Voice Code Scam

Buyer asks for your number to "verify," sends you a Google Voice code. They're claiming your number for fraud. Never share any verification code with a buyer.

Execution tip

Edit your listing price exactly on the scheduled day — don't wait. Marketplace algorithms treat price edits as "recently updated" and show your item to fresh buyers. A Sunday evening price drop gets peak weekend traffic.

Price Drop Rules

✓ Drop in 10–15% steps — big enough to trigger re-engagement, small enough to protect value
✓ Drop on the same day of the week — Sunday evening sees peak buyer activity
✓ Wait until a negotiation is fully dead before dropping — never drop mid-deal
✓ Re-post after a big drop — fresh listing visibility = fresh buyer eyes
✗ Never drop more than twice in one week — signals desperation and invites lowball offers

🗓️ Sell It in 7 Days

A day-by-day action plan personalized to your item and start date.
🎯

The single most important rule

List at the right price on Day 1. Use the Pricing Calculator — an overpriced listing wastes all 7 days. Start right, close fast.

The Standard 7-Day Strategy

Day 1 — List It Right

Create & Post

  • Calculate your price
  • Take 8–12 photos
  • Write optimized title + description
  • Post on 2–3 platforms
Days 2-3 — Respond Fast

Qualify Buyers

  • Reply to ALL messages within 1 hr
  • Ask qualifying questions
  • Schedule first pickups
  • Hold your price — no negotiating yet
Day 4 — Diagnose

Check Performance

  • <5 views → title/photo problem
  • Views but no messages → price too high
  • Messages but no sale → qualify better
  • Adjust if needed
Day 5 — Weekend Push

Drive Traffic

  • Re-share listing to groups
  • Edit listing (triggers re-index)
  • Schedule weekend pickups
  • Post in local FB groups
Days 6-7 — Close It

Weekend Peak

  • Most sales happen Sat–Sun
  • Be available and fast on replies
  • Execute pickups
  • If unsold: drop price 15%
Day 8+ — Regroup

Next Steps

  • Sold → repeat with next item
  • Unsold → drop 15%, relist fresh
  • Still unsold → try a new platform
  • 3 weeks unsold → donate or store
