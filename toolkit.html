🚨 Common Scam Patterns to Know

The Overpayment Check

Buyer sends a check for more than the price, asks you to deposit it and send back the difference. Check bounces days later — you lose both the item and the money.

The Fake Payment Screenshot

Buyer sends a Venmo/PayPal screenshot that looks real. Never release an item based on a screenshot — only what shows in your actual account.

The Shipping Scam

Buyer says they'll pay shipping but needs you to ship first. You ship, payment never arrives. Always collect full payment before shipping anything.

The "Google Voice" Scam

Buyer asks for your phone number to "verify," sends you a Google Voice code. They're actually claiming YOUR phone number for fraud. Never share verification codes.

💡 How to execute a price drop

Edit your listing and lower the price exactly on the scheduled day. Most marketplace algorithms treat price edits as "recently updated" and show your listing to fresh buyers. Always drop at the scheduled time — not when buyers pressure you.

📊 Price Drop Principles

  • ✓ Drop in steps of 10-15% — big enough to trigger re-engagement, small enough to protect value
  • ✓ Drop on the same day each week — Sunday evening drops see the most engagement
  • ✓ Never drop when someone is actively negotiating — wait until the deal dies before dropping
  • ✓ Re-post after a big price drop — fresh listing = fresh visibility
  • ✗ Don't drop more than 2× in one week — signals desperation, invites lowball offers

🗓️ Sell It in 7 Days

A proven day-by-day action plan. List Monday, sold by Sunday.

🎯 The Most Important Rule

List it at the right price on Day 1 — use the Pricing Calculator to set a fair, data-backed price. An overpriced listing wastes all 7 days. Start right and close fast.

Universal 7-Day Strategy

Day 1 — Monday

List It Right

  • Calculate your price
  • Take 8-12 photos
  • Write optimized title + description
  • Post to 2-3 platforms
Day 2-3 — Tue/Wed

Respond Fast

  • Reply to ALL inquiries within 1 hr
  • Qualify buyers with 1-2 questions
  • Schedule first pickups
  • Don't negotiate — hold price
Day 4 — Thursday

Check & Adjust

  • Review views and message count
  • <5 views? Problem with title/photos
  • 5+ views, 0 messages? Price too high
  • Many messages, no sale? Negotiate too early
Day 5 — Friday

Push Weekend Traffic

  • Re-share listing to groups
  • Update listing (edit triggers re-index)
  • Schedule weekend pickups
  • Post in local Facebook groups
Day 6-7 — Weekend

Close It

  • Most sales happen Sat-Sun
  • Be flexible on pickup windows
  • If no sale: drop price 15%
  • Re-post as "Price Dropped"
Day 8+ — Next Step

Repeat or Regroup

  • Sold? Repeat with next item
  • Unsold? Drop 15% and re-list
  • Still unsold? Try a different platform
  • 3 weeks unsold? Donate + move on
