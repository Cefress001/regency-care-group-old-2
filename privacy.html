Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what SellerKit Pro does with your information. SellerKit Pro is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], located at [BUSINESS ADDRESS].

The short version: everything you type into the toolkit stays in your own browser. We do not have a server that stores your items, prices, or listings. We never see them. The one thing that does leave your browser is an automatic report when something breaks, described in section 5, and it is built to leave out what you typed.

1. What stays on your device

Every tool in the SellerKit Pro toolkit saves your inputs to localStorage in your own browser. That includes item names, retail prices, conditions, descriptions, measurements, photo checklists, your sale tracker, and everything else you enter.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we have no way to read it. If you clear your browser data, use a different browser, or switch devices, that information is gone — we cannot recover it for you.

2. What we receive when you buy

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our systems — we do not see, receive, or store full card numbers.

From Stripe we receive the information needed to fulfil and support your order:

  • Your name and email address
  • The last four digits and brand of your card
  • Your billing country and postal code
  • The amount, date, and status of the transaction

We use this only to deliver your purchase, answer support questions, and process refunds. Stripe's own handling of your data is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

3. If you email us

When you contact support@sellerkit.io we receive your email address and whatever you write. We keep support correspondence so we can handle follow-ups and refund requests, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

4. Third parties that see your request

The checkout page loads Stripe's payment script, and the home page, toolkit and post-purchase page can send error reports to Sentry (section 5). Contacting either service reveals your IP address and browser details to it, as it would on any site using them. Our typeface is served from our own site, not from Google Fonts.

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party marketing pixels.

5. Error reporting and analytics

When a page on this site hits a software error, it sends a report to Sentry, an error-monitoring service, so we can find and fix the problem. The home page, the toolkit and the post-purchase page do this; the checkout page does not.

A report contains the error message and where in our code it happened, the address of the page (with access codes and keys removed), your browser and device type, and a short list of what happened just before the error: which pages or tabs were opened and which buttons were clicked, identified by their place in the page rather than by any text. Reports are only sent when an error occurs, not while you use the site normally.

Reports are built to leave out what you type into the toolkit, your saved items, your sale tracker, and any AI key you have connected: none of them is collected on purpose. Your key is stripped from every report, and requests to your AI provider are not recorded at all. Sentry is told not to store your IP address.

SellerKit Pro does not use any analytics or visitor-tracking service. If we add one, this page will be updated before it goes live.

6. Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe sets cookies necessary for payment processing and fraud prevention on the checkout page. The toolkit uses browser localStorage, described in section 1, rather than cookies.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the payment, email and error-reporting providers described above, or where we are legally required to.

8. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@sellerkit.io and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. We honour these requests regardless of where you are.

To delete the data held in your own browser, clear your browser's site data for this site, or use the reset buttons inside each tool.

9. Data retention

Purchase records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes. Support emails are deleted when the matter is resolved and no longer needed. Error reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

10. Children

SellerKit Pro is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SellerKit Pro changes. The "last updated" date above will change when we do. If we begin collecting information we do not collect today — for example, if we introduce optional accounts with cloud sync — we will update this page before that feature launches.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: support@sellerkit.io
